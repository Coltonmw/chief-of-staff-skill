# Chief of Staff skill

A free, reusable Chief of Staff skill for AI agents that work for a small firm's principal. It helps an agent keep projects moving, run meetings, own weekly reporting, and carry client conversations up to the point of commitment, while leaving every price, term and send decision with the principal. ## What it does

The skill gives an agent one clear job: keep the firm's work moving so the principal can spend time on the decisions only they can make. It covers:

* Telling the story of current work to clients, teammates and newcomers
* Pitching only the offerings the principal has defined
* Handling client questions, feedback and concerns with facts
* Negotiating inside a written envelope the principal sets, and never committing to a price, term or contract without the principal's yes
* Running meetings and sending a private summary within 24 hours
* Keeping one project list with owners, next steps, due dates and risks
* Encouraging the team with specific recognition and no false urgency
* Owning weekly reports that lead with decisions needed
* Alerting the principal when something needs them, with what to do, why, and by when

The `weekly-reports.md` file lists report ideas and marks three to start with.

## How to install

Copy the `chief-of-staff` folder into your agent's skills directory. The agent reads `chief-of-staff/SKILL.md` when the work calls for it. Edit the guardrails and templates to fit your firm, and keep a changelog of what you change.
